## What must NOT go in here

Marketing photographs and renders taken from builder or project websites
(Homeland, Green Lotus, Suncity, TDI, Omaxe and the rest) are copyrighted by
those developers. They cannot be used on this site — a commercial brokerage
site — without written permission, and publishing another developer's
project photography also implies a representation relationship that may not
exist.

The right source for project imagery is the client's own photography of the
properties they actually represent.
